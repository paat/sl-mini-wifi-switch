First version. Check the Mini, turn its Wi-Fi off, or turn Wi-Fi on with your own name and password. A QR code is shown. When Android asks, tap Save, and the phone then joins that Wi-Fi by itself (on some phones you approve a notification instead). Picture guides, a Quick Settings tile, and offline reset help are included. Works offline and does not track you. The local API was checked by hand on one Mini; this app is not yet tried on a real Mini. Report problems on GitHub.
