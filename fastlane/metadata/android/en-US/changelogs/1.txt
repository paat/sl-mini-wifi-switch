First version. Check the Mini, turn its Wi-Fi off, or turn Wi-Fi on with your own name and password. A QR code is shown and your phone joins that Wi-Fi. Picture guides, a Quick Settings tile, and offline reset help are included. Works offline and does not track you. The way the app talks to the Mini was checked by hand on one Starlink Mini, but the app itself has not yet been tried on a real Mini, so please report problems on GitHub.
