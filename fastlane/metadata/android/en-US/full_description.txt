Mini Wi-Fi Switch is a simple app for people who use a Starlink Mini.

Right after you reset the Mini, you can:

- See whether the Mini is ready.
- Turn the Mini's Wi-Fi off, and send the internet through a cable to your home router.
- Turn Wi-Fi on with a name and password you choose. The app shows a QR code, and your phone joins that Wi-Fi on its own.
- Follow picture guides, including how to reset the Mini when you have no internet.
- Open the app from a Quick Settings tile.

You can use the app with no internet connection. It does not track you. It has no ads. A Wi-Fi password stays on your phone. It is not sent to anyone else.

This first version has been tested on one Starlink Mini. Some steps still need more real-world testing.
