Mini Wi-Fi Switch is a simple app for people who use a Starlink Mini.

It is meant for one job, right after you reset the Mini. You choose one of two things:

- Turn the Mini's Wi-Fi off, and send the internet through a cable to your home router.
- Turn Wi-Fi on with a name and password you choose.

You can use the app with no internet connection. It does not track you. It has no ads. A Wi-Fi password stays on your phone. It is not sent to anyone else.

This project is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by Starlink or SpaceX. Starlink is a trademark of SpaceX. The name is used here only to say which dish the app is for.

The guided steps for those two choices are not in this version yet. This version has the app icon and a short opening screen.
